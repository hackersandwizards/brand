# Signature phrases: Benedikt's real vocabulary

The calibration anchors are measured from real sent mail and from his LinkedIn export; the email
exemplars are read from the mailbox live. The slogan catalogue below is not: his meeting and mail
vocabulary is far plainer than the catalogue implies, so use it to recognise his positioning, never
to generate his sentences. Slogans attested in written material live in `brand-vocabulary.md`.

## Calibration anchors

Five sentences that are pure user voice:

1. *"Bekommen wir alles hin."*
2. *"Profi. Pre-Mortem ist super geil! Danke dir [team member] :)"*
3. *"Hui. Den Termin hab ich total verpeilt weil er nicht im Kalender steht."*
4. *"Vermisse euch etwas :)"*
5. *"Wird sich zeigen. Nur schon mal als Info."*

LinkedIn DM and post anchors, split at 2021/2022 because the register moved. Copy-pasted outreach
templates count once in every number below, and a phrasing that lives only in a template is not an
anchor. DM numbers read 2017-21 / 2022-24.

- DM length: median 9 / 11 words, p90 32 / 40. 67% / 59% under 15 words (54% with the templates
  counted), 0.7% / 1.3% at 100 or more. Thread openers median 23 / 24 words.
- Post length: median 4.5 / 15 words, p90 25 / 57, longest 179. A third of the 2017-21 posts are
  one word (`Neat.` `Hammer` `Insane!`); 13% of 2022-24 posts reach 50 words. Up to 2024 his posts
  are share captions, not essays; the essay register starts with the 2025 post pipeline.
- One message carries the whole turn. 27% / 19% of his turns are more than one message inside
  three minutes (mean 1.45 / 1.26), against 62% in the messaging corpus in `personal-voice.md`.
- Openings. No greeting on 84% / 75% of all DMs, because most are mid-thread: replies open bare
  90% / 85% of the time. Thread openers (nothing in the conversation for seven days, n=154 / 519):
  `Moin` 46% / 55%, `Hey` 27% / 25%, bare 25% / 20%, `Hi` 1%, `Hallo` under 1%, name-first under
  1%. `Moin` carries the first name 94% / 96%, `Hey` 83% / 85%; `Moin ihr beiden` 13, `Moin
  zusammen` 0. `Hey` does not sort by warmth (a warmth question follows `Hey` 17% and `Moin` 15%).
  Elongated openers (`Heyyy`, `Naaa`, `Sooo`) 16 / 61. A first message after a connection request
  opens `Danke fürs Annehmen` 1 / 28 and a reply to one `Danke für die Anfrage` 8 / 17.
- Closings. No sign-off on 56% / 54% of all DMs; a sign-off on 45% / 61% of thread openers and 7% /
  12% of replies. `/bene` 1,001 against `/benedikt` 107 and `/benedict` 13. `LG und ...` warm
  variants 4 / 10 in total. A smiley is the closer on 29% / 21% (`:)`, `:P`, `:D`), `Danke` on 3.5%
  / 2.9%. Posts end on `!` 50% / 41%, on `:)` 14% / 14%, on `?` 0% / 8%.
- Language. DMs German 80% / 75%, English 4% / 14%, the rest one-liners that carry neither. Posts
  German 47% / 51%, English 8% / 46%.
- Emoji: 0.004 / 0.005 per DM, on 0.3% / 0.5% of DMs. ASCII instead: 49% / 41% of DMs carry `:)`
  (30% / 29%), `:P` (9% / 6%), `:D` (4% / 4%), `xD` (6% / 2%) or `:(` `:/` (5% / 10%). Posts 0 /
  0.31 per post, on 13% of 2022-24 posts, mostly conference and event posts.
- Question in 28% / 32% of DMs, 11% end on one; exclamation in 18% / 26%. Posts: question 8% /
  15%, exclamation 61% / 57%.
- `Sie` or `Ihnen` in 0.16% / 0.27%. Em-dash 0 in 5,910 DMs, 2 in posts. `bitte` 13 / 19. `gern`
  121 / 348 against `gerne` 6 / 18, `grad` 82 / 144 against `gerade` 1 / 8, `vllt` 40 / 105 against
  `vielleicht` 2 / 12, `ggf.` 18 / 34, `bissi` 15 / 17, `nen` 41 / 94.
- A DM that asks (hand-written, 15+ words, a question and an ask form; n=59 / 223): greets 75% /
  74%. The sentence after the greeting is context 54% / 50%, a warmth question 19% / 37%, the ask
  itself 27% / 14%. The ask sits in the middle 48% / 46%, last 25% / 41%, first 27% / 14%. What
  follows it is more context 66% / 52%: the mechanics or a stake-lowering tail, a lunch instead of
  a call, `oder so?`, or the booking link in the same message (10% / 23%). Signed 58% / 66%. So:
  greeting, warmth question in a third, one or two sentences of why now, `Hast du Lust ...?` or
  `Wollen wir ...?`, the tail, sign-off.
- A DM that declines (n=31 / 68; nearly all scheduling, five in seven years refuse a proposition):
  `leider` in 68% / 78%, the decline is the first sentence 65% / 46%, a bare `Ne.` / `Nein.` opener
  once in 99. The reason follows as its own sentence (a `weil` / `da` in 6%), an alternative slot
  or window in 36% / 41%, a smiley in 42% / 46%, `sorry` in 6% / 1.5%. Median 21 / 32 words.

Ten recurring DM phrasings, by frequency, hand-written count 2017-21 / 2022-24:

2. `Freue mich!` as its own sentence, 0 / 68.
3. `Ich melde mich`, 21 / 46; `Ich melde mich die Tage` 1 / 8.
4. `Sagmal, ...` launching the ask, 15 / 40.
5. `... oder so?` ending the ask, 23 / 22.
6. `Lieb, dass du ...`, 13 / 15, of which `Lieb, dass du schreibst!` 7 / 8.
7. `An welche Email schicke ich den Termin?`, 6 / 22.
8. `Such dir gern hier was bei mir raus` plus a booking link, 0 / 20.
9. `Kein Stress`, 5 / 15, and `Keine Eile` 2 / 7.
10. `Machen wir so!`, 3 / 15.

Next at 17 each: `Wann passt es dir denn?` 8 / 9, `Freue mich riesig` 2 / 15, `Mir geht es
super` 2 / 15. Quick-reply tokens the mail corpus lacks: `Mega!` 5 / 31, `Top` 7 / 26. Only three
post phrasings recur three times: `Freue mich auf euch!`, `It has been a blast`, `Was meint ihr?`,
all 2023-24.

## Real email exemplars

Read them from Gmail; do not store them here. Before a draft that needs voice depth:

```bash
gws gmail users messages list --params '{"userId":"me","q":"in:sent -label:os/drafted to:ADDRESS","maxResults":5}'
gws gmail users messages get --params '{"userId":"me","id":"ID","format":"full"}'
```

Drop the `to:` term for the register rather than the relationship, and add a language term where
the draft is English. Read the new text above the quote header, not the quoted thread.
`-label:os/drafted` excludes the mail an agent drafted for him.

## Positioning and identity

- *"Agents sind keine AI. Sie sind Programme, die AI nutzen."*
- *"AI ist ein Amplifikator. Gute Architektur skaliert schneller, schlechte Architektur wird
  schneller schlechter."* / *"AI amplifies what's already there. Good processes get better. Bad
  processes get worse faster."*
- *"This isn't a tool rollout. It's a change management initiative."*
- *"It's not about using AI, it's about developing it as a colleague."*
- *"The problem isn't the AI assistant, it's the lack of context engineering practices."*
- *"Die Technologie ist eigentlich gar nicht das, was im Weg steht. Im Weg steht ist dieser Change in
  den Leuten."*
- *"Die echten Effizienzgewinne holt man in den Prozessen drumherum."*
- *"Das ist nicht Vibe Coding... hier wird nichts gecodet und hier wird auch nichts gevibed."*
- *"Man kann mit dem AI-Thema unter dem Radar noch breitere organisatorische Veränderungen
  treiben."*
- *"Only a team that delivers the assessment itself with AI has the credibility to judge AI
  readiness. Anything else is hearsay."*
- Origin: *"Ich starte meine Story immer ganz gerne damit, dass ich 1999 den Computer von meinem
  Vater auseinandergebaut habe."* PC disassembly at age 9, Pentium 750MHz. *"The magic is real,
  it's just sufficiently advanced."* *"Reclaiming the magic in technology."*

## Methodology and craft

- *"Context is everything"* / *"Kontext ist alles"*.
- *"Die Lösung muss eigentlich immer schon in dem sein, was ich dem LLM schicke."* / *"The answer
  needs to be in the question."*
- *"70-80% preparation, 20-30% coding."*
- *"Smart Zone / Dumb Zone"* with the 60% threshold: at 60% fill, quality degrades invisibly.
- *"Instructions-Last"* / *"Bare Metal starten"*: start with zero context, let the agent discover
  what it needs.
- *"Research Plan Implement."* Fractal at every level: feature, story, task.
- *"Start simple. Observe. Refine."*
- *"Sprint 1-2: anstrengend. Sprint 3-4: breakeven. Sprint 5-6: schneller."*
- *"CLAUDE.md files are guidelines, not guarantees. For critical safety, use environment
  controls."*
- *"Manufacturing pipeline"*: industrial metaphor for SDLC.
- *"Kein Bauchgefühl, sondern klare Werte."*
- *"Die User-Story ist ein durchlaufendes Ding für die Planung. Die Spezifikation beinhaltet das, was
  nachher wirklich daraus entstanden ist."*
- *"Ich hab von diesen 80 Seiten genau drei gelesen."* [client] procurement story; Claude Code
  generated *"die beste Ausschreibung, die sie je gesehen haben."*

## Diagnostic, conviction, and self-awareness

- *"You have a pressure cooker situation."* The top-down/bottom-up gap.
- *"Productivity illusion"*: what false metrics produce.
- *"Ich habe keine Ahnung von AI."* Self-deprecation before pivoting to process expertise.
- *"Ich mach mir immer Sorgen, dass die alles schon können. Tun sie nie."*
- *"Ich denke ja immer beim Reden."*
- *"Unser FastAPI Repository ist natürlich super ordentlich."*
- *"Ehrlicherweise"*: real but rare. 44 occurrences, 0.36 per 1000 words, roughly 39x rarer than
  `also`. A marker, not a tic: *"ich benutze das Wort ehrlicherweise gerade ehrlicherweise relativ
  viel."*
- Blunt tool assessments, jokes about tools and not a model for disagreeing with a person
  (`postures.md` posture 2): *"Politisch korrekt ist der nicht gut, politisch unkorrekt ist der
  einfach scheisse."* (Copilot) / *"Alle Menschen, die VSCode nutzen, sind mir grundsaetzlich
  erstmal suspekt."* / *"Man braucht die IDE irgendwie nicht mehr so richtig."*

## Vision and empowerment

- *"180 Agenten-Teams hinter sich."* 2-3 person teams backed by hundreds of agent instances.
- *"Our job changes from writing code and reviewing code to reading what the AI produces."*
- *"Ich glaube, es macht wirklich total viel Sinn, wenn ihr einfach selber auch erstmal viel
  ausprobiert."*
- *"Defaults eines Modells sind quasi User-Feedback."*

## Qualified claims

*"That works great for [X], but if you're [Y], you need something different."* / *"SPAs sind
legacy"* / *"Microservices verhindern Geschwindigkeit"*. Conviction in delivery; in writing, attach
the *"in the right context"* qualifier so they don't read as absolutist.
