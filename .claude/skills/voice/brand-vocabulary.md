## Approved phrases

- Offerings: *"AI developer transformation program"* (commercial service), *"AI engineering
  leadership workshop"* (training).
- Value props: *"Replace random prompts with Agentic Engineering practices"*, *"From AI skeptics to
  AI craftsmen"*.
- Adjectives and themes: craft / craftsmanship, empowerment, mentor / mentorship (over "expert").
- Audience problem-language for unaware and problem-aware readers, their words before ours:
  *"Developer productivity declining"*, *"Managing technical debt from generative AI"*.

### Written positioning vocabulary

Attested in LinkedIn posts and the handbook, not in his speech or messages. Use it to recognise and
reinforce positioning in authored content; a drafted mail or Slack message never reaches for it.

- *"Non-negotiables"*: hard constraints an agent must never violate. 4 posts and 14 handbook and
  knowledge pages.
- *"Du bist der Navigator, er ist der Driver."* Pair-programming analogy for agentic work, 7 pages.
- *"Eventual Consistency for AI"*: reframe for architects worried about non-determinism, 1 post
  and 4 pages.
- *"Disposable software"*: AI makes throwaway code economically viable, 1 post.
- *"Agentic Coaching is the new Agile Coaching"*: the sales analogy clients grasp because they all
  lived through an agile transformation, 1 post.
- Diagnostic framings of the client's situation: *"Experimentation Trap"* (individuals experiment,
  the org makes no progress, 2 pages), *"Süppchen kochen"* (1 page), *"Stop being the lone hero who
  saves every sprint."* (1 page).

Carve-outs from the banned words: "Assistant" and "Assistants" are allowed for AI development tools
and human-assistance contexts; "Transformation" stays in the offering name above.

## Competitive positioning

h&w sits on the practices and enablement layer of the agentic-engineering stack, not in competition
with AI-coding tools:

- Tooling layer: Ona (formerly Gitpod), Blitzy, Cognition Devin, GitHub Copilot Workspace, Claude
  Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, sovereign models (Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama).
- h&w layer: pre-configured blueprints, training, embedded mentoring, community.
- Strategy layer, complementary: they build strategy, h&w executes operationally.

The real competitor is the client's in-house team trying to build the setup themselves; tools are
commodity, configuration and adoption are not. Never head-to-head against tool vendors in a pitch,
deck, post, application or speaker slot. Strategy firms are complementary, a client is never a
competitor, a delivery partner is never a competitor.

Firm name casing: preserve the firm's canonical capitalization even where its own employees write
it in lowercase in signatures or emails. The single exception is a verbatim quote from partner-side
material, such as a legal clause lifted from their template.

> speed their teams gain reaches their customers."*

with agentic engineering, as one team." German: "Wir bringen jede Rolle, die die Produkte unserer
Kunden baut, als ein Team in den Flow mit Agentic Engineering." It retires "thrive with AI, not despite it".
