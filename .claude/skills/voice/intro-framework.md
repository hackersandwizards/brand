# Introduction framework: NSFAG

Five-beat scaffold for 30-second self-introductions: networking, conference openers, podcast and
panel intros, *"Stell dich mal kurz vor"* moments. Skip when the host introduced you.
`[FIRST-NAME]` is whoever the draft is for; the examples are the shape, not one person's script.

- N, Name: *"[FIRST-NAME] from hackers&wizards"*. Role only if it adds immediate value.
  *"[FIRST-NAME] from hackers&wizards. I get engineering teams in flow with agentic
  engineering, so the speed they gain reaches their customers."*
- S, Same: the category everyone already understands, said plainly. *"We do consulting and training
  in agentic engineering."*
- F, Fame: one number plus one success story, result not title, under the numbers rule in
  `brand-voice.md`. *"I helped scale [client] from 200M to 1.2B in revenue."* / *"30+ teams
  ignited, 500+ developers in our community, 70+ practitioners in our network."*
- A, Aim: what you work on now (Priestley: the next 90 days), told as their benefit. *"I'm curious
  why some teams fly with AI while others crash."* / *"I help teams skip the AI mistakes I've seen a
  hundred times."* Never *"I want to grow my consultancy"*.
- G, Game: the bigger vision (Priestley: the next few years), then one next step toward it the
  listener can picture taking. *"Our big game: a whole organization in flow, every role that builds
  the product tuned to the others. So I'm asking three questions: Where are you now? Where do you
  want to be? What's blocking the path?"* / *"Coffee chat about your biggest technical headache?"*

The N stays constant; S-F-A-G swap per audience: a skeptical enterprise CTO cares about money and
risk, a startup founder about lean execution, a conference crowd about craft.
