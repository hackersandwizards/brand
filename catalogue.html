  • Logo for a slide or a document: lockup.svg, on dark lockup-night.svg, square lockup-compact.svg.

Logos

Three logos, each one line: the lockup is the standard, the wordmark alone goes on textiles and stickers, the compact lockup on small spots. The mail signature keeps the typed wordmark. On night, black, black textile and Stargazer take the night cut, without an outline; on Purple Love the outline cut, a white outline round the letters. Alignment rules: README.md, "Use it in another repo".

lockup

The standard logo: wizard beside the pixel wordmark. Every surface without its own row: screens, slides, documents, website, banners, notebook, cloth.
