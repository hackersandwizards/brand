Logos

Three logos, each one line: the lockup is the standard, the wordmark alone goes on textiles and stickers, the compact lockup on small spots. The mail signature keeps the typed wordmark. On night, black, black textile and Stargazer take the night cut, without an outline; on Purple Love and the gradient the outline cut, a white outline round the letters. Alignment rules: README.md, "Use it in another repo".

lockup

The standard logo: wizard beside the pixel wordmark. Every surface without its own row: screens, slides, documents, website, banners, notebook, cloth.

.svg.pngnight.svgnight.pngoutline.svgoutline.png

lockup-compact

Stacked, for small and square spots: mug, lock screens. The iPhone lock screen keeps it at its smaller size.

.svg.pngnight.svgnight.pngoutline.svgoutline.png

wordmark

The wordmark alone, for textiles: shirts, hoodie and stickers. Also where the wizard already appears nearby.
