Quick start

  • Logo for a document or a screen: lockup.svg, on night lockup-night.svg, on Purple Love, Stargazer or the gradient lockup-outline.svg; for small spots lockup-compact.svg. Which logo where: Logos.
  • Print: textiles and stickers take the wordmark alone, the second sticker the wizard alone; the print files are under Merch, the booth files under Booth banners.
  • Slides: there is no template file. Decks come from the slide engine, @hackersandwizards/slides; its slide types are on its specimen page, under Slides. Documents come from the document engine, @hackersandwizards/documents, under Documents.
  • Workshop kit for trainers: the lockup above, the night call background call-background.png (all backgrounds under Applications), slides from the slide engine.
  • In code: @import "@hackersandwizards/brand/tokens.css";, the rest in README.md.

Spacing, radius, shadow, layout

Corners and icons

The brand corner, --radius-brand, goes on every surface large enough to show it at its full value.

Smaller marks, a colour swatch or a list marker, are square, never a scaled corner.

Delivered

Icons are Lucide line icons in the colour of their text, never emoji. A kind of label carries an icon on every value or on none.

LinkedIn post images

Every post image, 1080 x 1350: the headline in white on the gradient band, the image as a card below it, the lockup on the tint. The package ships three commands: brand-linkedin-frame frames a photo, a sketch or a terminal; brand-linkedin-terminal renders the dark terminal window from a spec; brand-linkedin-sketch-prompt prints the prompt for a generated marker sketch and the worked examples, each sketch-example card here with the filled prompt that drew it.

Device stickers

The wordmark sticker on the back of every company device, centred left to right, below the Apple logo and upright like it. MacBook, every size: stickerD-D1 (140 x 25 mm), its centre 45 mm above the hinge edge, and above it the wizard sticker stickerZ-Z2 (67 x 76 mm), centred on the Apple logo. iPhone: stickerP-P10 (62 x 14 mm), its centre 22 mm above the bottom edge, below the MagSafe ring; on the case where the phone has one. iPad, in portrait: stickerB-B16 (96 x 19 mm), its centre 50 mm above the bottom edge. The print files are under Merch. Drawn to scale from Apple's dimensions: MacBook Pro 14-inch, iPad Pro 11-inch, each in silver and space black, and iPhone 18 Pro bare and in a case.

Mail signatures

One signature for every mail, new mails and replies alike, from one template with placeholders. From an h&w account: the typed wordmark in colour, name, role, contact line and the three lines § 35a GmbHG requires. From a trainer's own address: name, the role line "Freier Trainer für hackers&wizards" and the website, without wordmark and GmbH lines, which would present the trainer as part of the GmbH. `bun run brand-mail-signature` writes an HTML file to open in a browser and copy into the mail client, and a plain-text file for the iPhone. Links in teal #1A7285 (5.54:1 on white). Shown with a fictitious person.

Slides

Decks come from the slide engine, the package @hackersandwizards/slides; there is no template file. Its specimen shows one slide of every type. The slide logo: the lockup on the title and the closing slide, the wordmark alone on every other slide. A concept slide that claims a mechanism in a few short cards may carry a generated marker sketch beside them: brand-slide-sketch-prompt prints the prompt and the worked example, the sketch-example card here; the slides README, "Deck structure", says which slides get one.
