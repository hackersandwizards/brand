Quick start

  • Logo for a slide or a document: lockup.svg, on dark lockup-night.svg, square lockup-compact.svg.
  • Slides: there is no slide template file. Decks are built in the talks slide engine (styles/slides.css); its title, content and closing slides are under talks.
  • In code: @import "@hackersandwizards/brand/tokens.css";, the rest in README.md.
